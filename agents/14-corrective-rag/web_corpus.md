# Web fallback corpus — the broader "internet"

The corrective `web_search` action draws from here when the grader judges the
local retrieval insufficient. It deliberately covers facts that are NOT in the
local corpus (and does not duplicate local facts), so an over-eager grader that
routes a perfectly-good local hit to the web will be punished.

- Cael is a trade hub in the Sirin Reach, founded in 2176; its primary export is deuterium.
- The Vernal Compact, signed in 2188, governs the moons of the Sirin Reach.
- Ostrov Station is governed by Dr. Hadle Sun, who was appointed in 2205.
- The colony of Pell's Rest was abandoned in 2199 after a reactor failure.
- Marin Kuso founded the Sirin Reach trade routes and established Cael's first orbital dock.
- The deepest mine on Vex, the Korrin Shaft, reaches 3.2 kilometres below the surface.
- Tovik is a research colony in the Sirin Reach studying dark-matter currents; its lead scientist is Eda Pol.
